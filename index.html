More From Our Menu

Maple Oat Latte

Espresso and steamed oat milk sweetened with a spoonful of real maple syrup and topped with foam art.

Details
Our most popular order on cold mornings. We steam the oat milk until it is silky, then stir in the maple syrup before the espresso goes in.

Order the Maple Oat Latte

A Maple Oat Latte is ready in about three minutes. Order at the counter and tell our barista if you would like it iced.

Midnight Cold Brew

Coffee steeped slowly in cold water for a smooth, low-acid, deep flavour.

Details
Each batch steeps for sixteen hours in the back of the cafe. It is strong, so many students add a splash of milk or a little vanilla.

Order the Midnight Cold Brew

Midnight Cold Brew is poured over ice and served in a tall glass. Ask for a refill during your study session at a lower price.

Spiced Chai Latte

Black tea simmered with cinnamon, cardamom and ginger, topped with warm milk.

Details
We simmer our own spice blend every morning instead of using a syrup. The result is warm, fragrant and not too sweet.

Order the Spiced Chai Latte

Our Spiced Chai Latte can be made with dairy milk or oat milk. It is a great choice if you want something warm without much caffeine.

Blueberry Oat Muffin

A soft muffin packed with blueberries and finished with a crunchy oat topping.

Details
Our student bakers make these every morning with rolled oats and plenty of berries. They are best while they are still a little warm.

Order the Blueberry Oat Muffin

Blueberry Oat Muffins sell out by early afternoon. Reserve one at the counter before your first class if you want to be sure.

Smashed Avocado Toast

Toasted bread topped with smashed avocado, sliced radish and a boiled egg.

Details
A filling snack between lectures. We season the avocado with lemon juice and a pinch of sea salt, then finish it with sliced radish and a boiled egg.

Order the Smashed Avocado Toast

Smashed Avocado Toast is served on fresh bread. Ask the counter for an extra egg if you want a bigger meal.

Cinnamon Swirl Roll

A fluffy roll with a thick cinnamon swirl and sweet cream cheese icing.

Details
The dough rests overnight so it bakes up soft and light. We ice each roll while it is warm so the topping melts into the swirls.

Order the Cinnamon Swirl Roll

A Cinnamon Swirl Roll is big enough to share. Pair it with a Midnight Cold Brew for the cafe's favourite study combo.
